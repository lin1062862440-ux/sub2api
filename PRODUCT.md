# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sub2API is used by operators and developers who need to distribute, monitor, and bill access to upstream AI subscription capacity. They visit public auth pages to enter the console, then work in an admin or user dashboard where reliability, clarity, and fast task completion matter more than decorative brand expression.

## Product Purpose

Sub2API provides an AI API gateway for subscription quota distribution. It lets the platform generate API keys, route traffic to upstream accounts, track usage and costs, manage payments, and expose a web console for operational control.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, technical. The interface should feel trustworthy and direct, with enough polish to support a paid gateway product without turning operational screens into marketing pages.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, excessive teal decoration, oversized marketing cards, decorative motion, and generic AI platform copy. Public auth pages should not feel like a template landing page or a crypto dashboard.

## Design Principles

- Make the task obvious before adding expression.
- Use the configured product name and logo as the identity source of truth.
- Keep public entry pages restrained, readable, and fast to scan.
- Reserve strong color for primary actions and semantic states.
- Preserve operational confidence across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, native form semantics, clear loading and disabled states, and reduced reliance on color alone for validation. Motion should be subtle and not required to understand state.
